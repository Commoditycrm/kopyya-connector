Kopyya Connector

Connect your Discord to Kopyya

A small app that lets Kopyya follow your Discord trade alerts. You sign in to Discord on your own computer — your password never reaches Kopyya.

How to connect

  1. In Kopyya, go to Add channel → Connect Discord. You'll get a code like KPY-4F2A-9C1D. It works once and expires after 10 minutes.
  2. Open the Connector you downloaded, paste the code and click Connect.
  3. A browser window opens on Discord's login page. Sign in as you normally would, including any 2FA or verification Discord asks for.
  4. Done. The Connector says Connected and Kopyya starts following the channel. You can close the app.

Good to know

What it shares

  • Only the Discord session created after you sign in
  • Never your password or 2FA codes
  • Stored encrypted by Kopyya

What you need

  • Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge installed
  • Windows 10+ or macOS on Apple Silicon
  • A pairing code from your Kopyya account

Security warning on first launch

Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.
macOS: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.

Code not accepted?

Codes expire after 10 minutes and can only be used once. Generate a fresh one in Kopyya and try again.
